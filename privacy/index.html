Retirement Countdown

A focused, privacy-first dashboard that turns your retirement date into a live plan with real-time timers, milestones, widgets, and motivating quotes.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Retirement Countdown app and this website collect, use, and protect information. This is a generic policy intended as a starting point — replace or extend it to match your legal and product requirements.

Effective date: 2026-02-08

  1. Information We Collect
    • Local App Data: The Retirement Countdown app stores user data locally on the device (retirement date, settings, milestones, theme selection, and any optional user-provided content). We do not send or store this data on our servers by default.
    • Analytics & Third Parties: This website does not include analytics by default. If analytics or crash reporting is enabled in the future, it will be listed here with the specific provider and what is collected.
  2. How We Use Information
    • Local Use: Data stored by the app is used solely to provide app functionality (countdowns, widgets, milestone notifications, and preferences).
    • Website Use: Information submitted through the website (contact forms, if added) will be used to respond to inquiries and improve the service.
  3. Sharing and Disclosure
    • We do not sell or rent personal information. We will not share local app data with third parties unless you explicitly opt in or we are required by law.
  4. Cookies and Tracking
    • This site may use standard cookies for basic site functionality. No tracking or profiling is performed by default.
  5. Data Security
    • We take reasonable measures to protect information stored on our servers and transmitted to us. For the app, since data is stored locally, device security controls (lock screen, backups) apply.
  6. Third-Party Links
    • This website or app may include links to third-party services (app stores, payment providers). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services.
  7. Children
    • The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  8. Changes to This Policy
    • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will be updated when changes are made.
  9. Contact
    • For questions about this policy or requests concerning personal data, please contact the app support email listed on the store listing.
